# Product Assumptions

## 1. What product are we planning to build?

We are planning to build a web-based order management system for designer boutiques and custom dress-making businesses. It helps boutique administrators manage customer details, dress orders, measurements, delivery dates, and order progress. Customers can also track their orders through a dedicated customer portal.

## 2. What problem does it solve?

Many designer boutiques manage customer orders, measurements, alterations, and delivery updates manually. This can lead to confusion, difficulty tracking orders, and communication gaps between customers and boutique administrators.

OrderSnap solves these problems by providing a centralized platform to organize orders, monitor their progress, and manage customer alteration requests and feedback.

## 3. Who are the target users?

The main target users are:

- Designer boutique owners.
- Boutique administrators and staff.
- Tailors and dress designers.
- Customers who order customized dresses.

## 4. What is the main use case?

The main use case is managing customized dress orders from order creation to completion.

The administrator can add customer information, enter body measurements, specify dress details, upload reference photos, set delivery dates, and update order status.

Customers can search for their orders using their name and Order ID, view order details, check progress, and submit alteration requests and feedback.

## 5. What are the core features?

- Admin login and dashboard.
- Customer and dress order management.
- Customer contact details and body measurements.
- Dress type, colour, fabric, and design details.
- Reference image upload.
- Order ID generation.
- Order status tracking (Pending, In Progress, Completed).
- Delivery date and price management.
- Customer order tracking portal.
- Alteration request submission and management.
- Customer ratings and feedback.
- Order search and status filtering.
- Save order details as a draft.

## 6. What assumptions are we making about users?

- Boutique administrators have basic computer knowledge.
- Administrators can enter and update customer order details.
- Customers know their name and Order ID to access order information.
- Customers want to check their order progress without repeatedly contacting the boutique.
- Customers may need alterations after receiving or trying their dresses.
- Customers are willing to share feedback about the boutique's service.

## 7. What will be included in the MVP?

The Minimum Viable Product (MVP) will include:

- Basic admin login.
- Admin dashboard.
- Adding and managing customer orders.
- Recording customer measurements and dress specifications.
- Uploading dress reference images.
- Generating unique Order IDs.
- Updating order status and progress notes.
- Customer portal for searching and viewing orders.
- Alteration request submission.
- Customer feedback and rating system.

## 8. What will NOT be included in the MVP?

- Online payment and payment gateway integration.
- Automatic SMS, WhatsApp, or email notifications.
- Live delivery tracking.
- Inventory and fabric stock management.
- AI-based dress recommendations.
- Advanced sales and financial analytics.
- Mobile application for Android and iOS.
- Multi-branch boutique management.
- Integration with external delivery services.
